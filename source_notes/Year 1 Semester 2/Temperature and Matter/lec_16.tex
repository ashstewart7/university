\section{Atmosphere Continued}

We have established that the number density (number of fluid molecules per unit volume), at height 0, $\rho_N(0)$ can be related to atmospheric pressure by:
\[
    \rho_N(0) = \frac{P_\text{atmospheric}}{k_B T}
\]

To determine the total number of gas molecules in the slab of atmpshere with cross-sectional area $A$, we can integrate across all $h$:
\[
    N = A \int_{0}^{\infty} \rho_N(h) \, dh
\]
\[
    = A \int_{0}^{\infty} \rho_N(0) \exp\left(- \frac{mgh}{k_B T}\right) \, dh
\]
\[
    = A \rho_N(0) \int_{0}^{\infty} \exp\left(- \frac{mgh}{k_B T}\right) \, dh
\]
\[
    = A \rho_N(0) \left[- \frac{k_B T}{mg} \exp \left(- \frac{mhg}{k_B T}\right)\right]_0^\infty
\]
\[
    = A \rho_N(0) \frac{k_B T}{mg}
\]

Hence:
\[
    \rho_N(0) = \frac{Nmg}{A k_B T}
\]

And using:
\[
    h_0 = \frac{k_B T}{mg}
\]
\[
    \rho_N(0) = \frac{N}{A h_0}
\]
